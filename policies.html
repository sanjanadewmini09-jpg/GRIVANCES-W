Policy Code: RM-WHISTLE-01

Whistleblower Protection & Non-Retaliation Charter

RM Holdings (Pvt) Ltd is committed to the highest standards of financial, operational, and ethical integrity. This policy allows employees to report fraudulent activities, bribery, safety violations, or gross misconduct without fear of negative employment repercussions.

Total Identity Masking

In anonymous mode, our system strips your IP address, device metadata, and corporate user identity.

Zero Retaliation Guarantee

Any supervisor attempting retaliatory action against a whistleblower faces immediate dismissal.

Independent Panel

Severe reports are investigated by external legal counsel and the Managing Director.

Policy Code: RM-CONDUCT-03

Anti-Harassment, Dignity & Workplace Conduct

Every individual at RM Holdings is entitled to a workplace free from humiliation, verbal insults, sexual harassment, intimidation, and discriminatory bias based on gender, ethnicity, religion, or age.

• Verbal Abuse: Screaming, derogatory remarks during public meetings, or malicious slander are formal disciplinary violations.
• Sexual Harassment: Unwelcome physical conduct, inappropriate digital messages, or requests for sexual favors carry immediate suspension pending disciplinary inquiry.
• Psychological Safety: Constructive code reviews, feedback, and performance appraisals must be delivered with professional courtesy.

Policy Code: RM-REMUN-02

Fair Wages, Overtime & Working Hours

In strict compliance with the Sri Lankan Shop and Office Employees Act, RM Holdings guarantees fair calculation of overtime, statutory EPF (12% employer, 8% employee) and ETF (3%) contributions, and regulated working hours.

Overtime (OT) Regulations

Overtime is calculated at 1.5x hourly rate for normal working days, and 2.0x for Poya / Mercantile holidays. Mandatory overtime requires minimum 48 hours notice.

Study & Examination Leaves

Contractual study leaves for recognized certifications (CIMA, ACCA, SLIIT, Engineering licenses) are granted up to 5 days per calendar year.
