Simple 4-Step Process

How Grievances are Handled at RM Holdings

Compliant with Sri Lanka Labor regulations and international corporate ethics guidelines.

1

Submission

Employee submits a complaint through the portal or AI assistant. Choose between named or 100% anonymous disclosure.

2

Triage & Assignment

HR evaluates severity within 24 hours. A neutral Senior HRBP, Safety Officer, or Ethics Committee lead is formally assigned.

3

Impartial Inquiry

Confidential interviews, evidence analysis, and workplace audits are conducted while maintaining complete non-retaliation safeguards.

4

Resolution & Follow-up

Formal corrective action and resolution are shared transparently. The employee provides feedback before the case is closed.

Grievances Addressed Under Company Policy

🛡️

Harassment & Misconduct

Verbal abuse, bullying, sexual harassment, discriminatory treatment, or intimidation.

💵

Payroll & Compensation

Salary delays, overtime calculation disputes, EPF/ETF contributions, uncredited bonuses.

⚠️

Health, Safety & Facilities

Factory hazards, faulty machinery, ventilation, chemical handling, emergency exit blocks.

⏰

Workload & Working Hours

Excessive mandatory overtime, unfair shift rotas, leave cancellations, burnout conditions.

⚖️

Whistleblower & Ethics

Bribery, supplier favoritism, corporate fraud, theft, or breaches of commercial confidentiality.

📈

Appraisals & Career Growth

Unfair performance ratings, denial of contractual study leave, promotion bias.
